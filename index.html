Projects

TURING-EU

Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Applied & Computational Mathematics, FORTH, in the TURING (Trustworthy Unified Robust Intelligent Generative Systems) project, funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (Grant Agreement No. 101215032; 2025–2028).

The project develops robust AI-driven methods and generative models for scientific and engineering applications.

ADAPTSOL-HFRI

Principal Investigator of the Adaptive methods for solving PDEs (ADAPTSOL) project, supported by the Hellenic Foundation for Research and Innovation (H.F.R.I.) under the "2nd Call for H.F.R.I. Research Projects to Support Postdoctoral Researchers", hosted by the Institute of Applied & Computational Mathematics, FORTH (Project No. 1247; 2021-2025)

The ADAPTSOL project developed and analysed numerical methods for partial differential equations, drawing on adaptive approximation and variational approaches. Particular emphasis was placed on a posteriori error estimation and multiscale wavelet techniques, and on the analysis of residual-based and constrained variational formulations for transport and kinetic problems, with applications to neural-network approximation.
